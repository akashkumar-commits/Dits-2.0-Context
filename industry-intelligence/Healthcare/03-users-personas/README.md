
# Healthcare Users and Personas

## Purpose
Identify the audiences a healthcare organization serves and determine their goals, tasks, information needs, trust requirements, accessibility needs, and likely website journeys.

## Core principle
Personas represent user roles and needs, not assumptions about an individual based on age, gender, income, or other demographic attributes.

## How the intelligence unit should use this layer

1. Identify the healthcare business segment.
2. Identify the business model and services offered.
3. Determine which audiences are relevant to that specific customer.
4. Identify each audience's primary goals and tasks.
5. Map those tasks to website pages, features, content, and calls to action.
6. Determine whether the journey is informational, transactional, clinical, administrative, or a combination.
7. Identify privacy, accessibility, trust, and authorization requirements.
8. Ask the customer to confirm high-impact assumptions before finalizing the experience.

## Required output

For each relevant persona, produce:
- Persona role and description
- Evidence status
- Primary goals
- User tasks
- Information requirements
- Pain points to validate
- Trust and accessibility needs
- Website journey
- Relevant pages and features
- Primary call to action
- Data sensitivity
- Required access level
- Unanswered questions

## Evidence labels

- customer_confirmed: directly confirmed by the customer
- research_supported: supported by relevant research
- source_supported: supported by a traceable source
- industry_pattern: a common pattern that may apply
- hypothesis: a proposed assumption requiring validation
- unknown: insufficient information

## Rules
- Do not assume every hospital offers online appointment booking.
- Do not assume every patient has a caregiver or wants one involved.
- Do not assume a caregiver can access a patient's records.
- Do not assume a clinician can provide consultations through the website.
- Do not treat a persona as proof of user behavior.
- Do not invent patient research, interview findings, or conversion statistics.
- Do not use sensitive personal data to infer a persona unnecessarily.
- Do not allow persona definitions to determine clinical decisions.
