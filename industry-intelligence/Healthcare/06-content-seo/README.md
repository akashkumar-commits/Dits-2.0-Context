
# Healthcare Content and SEO Intelligence

## Purpose
Enable DITS 2.0 agents to plan, generate, review,
optimize, migrate, and maintain healthcare website
content responsibly.

## Scope
- Content strategy
- Content inventory and classification
- Healthcare content types
- Search intent
- On-page SEO
- Local SEO
- Structured data
- Content quality and evidence
- Medical and regulatory claim safeguards
- Content migration
- Search performance monitoring

## Required inputs
- Healthcare segment
- Business model and objectives
- Primary audiences
- Services or products
- Supported locations and markets
- Approved source material
- Existing website content, if available
- Brand voice and language requirements
- Target search markets
- Content ownership and approval process

## Required outputs
- Content inventory
- Content gaps
- Proposed page topics
- Content briefs
- Draft content
- Search intent mapping
- Metadata recommendations
- Internal linking recommendations
- Structured data candidates
- Evidence and claim review status
- Migration content mapping
- Approval requirements

## Mandatory rules
1. Do not invent clinical facts or medical advice.
2. Do not invent services, providers, credentials,
   locations, certifications, or outcomes.
3. Do not invent keyword volumes or rankings.
4. Do not claim regulatory compliance without evidence.
5. Do not fabricate patient reviews or testimonials.
6. Use verified customer and authoritative sources.
7. Distinguish marketing language from factual claims.
8. Require qualified review for clinical content.
9. Preserve important existing content during migration
   unless removal or replacement is approved.
10. Never guarantee search rankings or traffic growth.
