
# Healthcare Compliance and Security Intelligence

## Purpose
Help agents identify applicable obligations, protect sensitive
information, plan appropriate safeguards, and recognize when
qualified human review is required.

## Scope
- Jurisdiction identification
- Privacy and data protection
- Sensitive data handling
- Security architecture
- Consent and privacy notices
- Accessibility obligations
- Medical device regulatory boundaries
- Incident response
- Human review and approval

## Required inputs
- Organization type
- Services and products
- Countries and regions served
- Website and portal functionality
- Categories of data collected
- Data subjects and intended users
- Systems and third parties involved
- Hosting and data-transfer arrangements
- Applicable contracts and existing policies
- Customer's compliance owner

## Required outputs
- Jurisdiction and applicability assessment
- Data inventory
- Risk and control checklist
- Third-party data flow inventory
- Required notices and consent questions
- Regulatory review requirements
- Security validation requirements
- Unresolved questions
- Approval and evidence records

## Mandatory rules
1. Do not claim that a website is legally compliant without evidence.
2. Do not assume all healthcare organizations have identical obligations.
3. Do not treat consent as the only possible lawful basis for processing.
4. Do not assume a privacy policy alone makes processing lawful.
5. Do not send sensitive information to unapproved third parties.
6. Do not expose credentials, tokens, or sensitive records.
7. Do not make legal or regulatory determinations beyond verified evidence.
8. Escalate uncertain or high-impact questions to qualified reviewers.
9. Record the source, jurisdiction, version, and review date for requirements.
10. Block unsafe publication or deployment when mandatory controls fail.
